# Product

## Register
Brand — the site IS the brand. A luxury travel lifestyle landing page for a couple building an influencer following. Design communicates personality; every visitor interaction should feel like an invitation.

## Who
Aspirational travelers, food lovers, and lifestyle followers (25–45) who dream of slow travel, elevated meals, and escaping the 9-to-5. They find the brand on Instagram or through SEO and arrive wanting to be inspired — and to follow along on the journey.

## Purpose
Build an audience and establish Passports & Prosecco as a recognizable, followable luxury travel brand. Convert visitors to Instagram followers, email subscribers, and eventually brand-partnership leads.

## Desired Outcome
Visitors leave feeling: *"I wish that was my life — I need to follow them."* They subscribe, follow, and come back.

## Brand Personality
**Elevated · Joyful · Authentic**

Not the stiff pretense of a 5-star hotel brochure. Not the frenetic chaos of a TikTok travel channel. Something in between: a couple who traded spreadsheets for cobblestones, who orders the second bottle, who believes a great meal deserves a great setting. Pinky up, but laughing while they do it.

## Voice
- Warm, first-person plural ("we"). Never distant.
- Wit without try-hard. A raised eyebrow, not a punchline.
- The details matter: not "a great view" but "the kind of terrace that makes you forget what day it is."
- Tagline foundation: *"Keep your pinky up, darling."*

## Anti-References
- Over-produced luxury hotel aesthetics (too stiff, too corporate — we're people, not a brand campaign)
- TikTok chaos (too many fonts, neon flash, zero breathing room)
- Cold minimalist startup white (clean but lifeless)
- Generic travel blog beige (the AI-cream monoculture)

## Site Sections (launch)
1. Nav (sticky, logo-centered or left, minimal links)
2. Hero (full-bleed, dramatic — establishing mood, tagline)
3. About Us (our story: quit the jobs, living the dream)
4. Travel Destinations (stats + featured places)
5. Food & Dining (because "Incredible Food" is half the brand)
6. Blog / Stories (latest posts preview)
7. Instagram Feed (social proof section)
8. Newsletter (email capture)
9. Work With Us (brand partnership CTA)
10. Footer

## Design Principles
1. **Darkness is the luxury.** Deep noir background. The brand lives in shadow and candlelight, not daylight-gray.
2. **Gold is the voice, rose is the warmth.** Two accent colors only — champagne gold and blush rose. Nothing else gets color.
3. **Photography first.** The destinations and food are the product. Images dominate; copy supports.
4. **One script, used sparingly.** The flowing italic Prosecco treatment is the brand's signature. Use it twice — never more.
5. **Motion is champagne bubbles.** Subtle upward reveals, nothing that bounces.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA minimum. All text on dark backgrounds must hit 4.5:1. Reduced-motion fallback on all scroll-triggered animations.
